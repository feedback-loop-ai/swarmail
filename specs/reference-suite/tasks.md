## Delivery ledger (implement after review return 1, 2026-11-19)

The review panel returned the run with three medium findings. Each is fixed on this branch; the lows are handled as noted.

- **A suite that claims nothing (guarantees, medium; correctness and independent, low and info).** The holder's rule is that a suite which started nothing never opened its window. `run_suite` now admits the first claim before it opens the window. If the cutoff leaves no claim (`CLAIM_FLOOR` before the earlier of the suite deadline and the chunk's work end), it returns every id unstarted, with its reason and no window. The holder seals that as the target's loss (exit 1). Once the window is open, the first claim is always made. If the holder's own round trip used up the claim's margin, the suite refuses and the chunk exits 2. `suite::check_record` refuses a record that started nothing but carries a window. Proofs: `claims_stop_at_the_chunks_end_and_the_suites_cutoff_with_every_id_unstarted` now asserts no window, and `the_holders_control_lines_are_closed_ordered_and_bound_to_its_window` takes an all-unstarted record through `windows_agree`, `check_against`, `cleanup_proved` and `failures`.
- **The 30 s and 540 s bounds (guarantees, medium; correctness, low).** Every test now ends inside the earlier of 30 s from its claim and the suite cutoff. The trial is cancelled `CANCEL_MARGIN` (1 s) inside that bound. Its cleanup deadline is `SETTLE_GRACE` (0.5 s) before the cancellation, and its work stops `CLEANUP_RESERVE` (3 s) before the cleanup deadline. A row's end is taken when the trial returns, before the fixture's drain. `check_record` refuses a row longer than `TEST_DEADLINE_MS`, a claim at or after `SUITE_DEADLINE_MS` from the first claim, and an end past it. Proof: new refusals in `a_record_is_checked_whole_and_refused_one_corrupted_field_at_a_time`, with the exact bound accepted.
- **A timed-out wait on a poll target (independent, medium).** The poll wait now stops one `REQUEST_BOUND` before the work deadline, as the await and the stream already do. The cleanup runs in its own reserved tail, after `NativeSource::extend_deadline`. A scoring step that fails after the solution has already failed no longer turns the row into `harness`; the oracle's typed failure classifies it. Proof: `a_poll_wait_that_times_out_is_a_timeout_row_that_still_removes_its_leftovers`. On a delete-by-id poll store whose search never finds the delivered mail, both rows are `timeout`, cleanup is `Done` with the leftovers removed by id, nothing store-wide runs, and each row ends inside its bound.
- **Lows.** The unavailable-policy row is now executed: `an_unavailable_cleanup_policy_seals_rows_that_record_no_cleanup` covers MailHog at 1, 8 and 32, and other dispositions are refused under that policy. Stale docs are fixed: the README's two "the testexec section is owed" passages and the draft's `change_reason`. The README now states that `interference` is 0 by construction in a real suite, and that the 540 s suite deadline cannot bind inside a 480 s chunk. Left as tracked debt: `Station::install` still runs at the claim, not before the suite clock (0132 §8.2's letter, low). There is no explicit late-delivery field (AC5, low). `timeout`, `wrong_message` and `interference` rows are proved through `classify` and, for `timeout`, now through `run_suite`, but there is no end-to-end `wrong_message` row (low).

## Delivery ledger (host repair, 2026-11-19)

The dispatcher's runs of `api_section::testexec_real_mailpit` failed 4 of 4. Each time one trial (129, 131, 137 or 192) had a scored pass, but its cleanup failed with `recover: operation: scan: incomplete` or `verify: operation: scan: incomplete`, so the row was `harness`.

- **Cause.** Under `delete_by_id`, Mailpit's cleanup recovers and verifies this test's mail with a store-wide offset-paged listing, `GET /api/v1/messages`. The captured Swagger (`docs/evals/sources/mailpit/mailpit-swagger.json`) gives `/api/v1/search` a `query`, but its syntax sits behind `https://mailpit.axllent.org/docs/usage/search-filters/`, which the lock does not capture. No recipient filter is cited, so none is used (0132 §8.3, §9.3). At 8 workers the other seven deliver and delete while the listing is read. Its stated total, `messages_count`, then moves between pages or within one answer (the captured `storage.List` selects the page apart from the count). The shared pager refuses such a listing as `Incomplete`. That refusal is right, and it is unchanged.
- **Fix.** `NativeSource::owned_listing` (`evals/src/native_source.rs`) is now the read for every cleanup policy's recovery and check. Where the listing is store-wide (`Client::scope_free(None)`, Mailpit only) and the suite runs above parallelism 1 (`beside_neighbours`, set from the coordinate), an `Incomplete` listing is read again, `RESCAN_PAUSE` (10 ms) apart, until one is complete. The pager accepts only a complete listing. It stops at the test's own cleanup deadline, inside the existing `CANCEL_MARGIN`, `SETTLE_GRACE` and `CLEANUP_RESERVE` bounds. A listing still moving then fails as `scan: incomplete (<n> listing(s) moved while read, then <cause>)`. Any other error fails at once, and so does a moving listing at parallelism 1. Only reads repeat: no delete runs on a refused listing, and nothing is escalated. Inbucket and GreenMail read per mailbox, and smtp4dev searches by the recipient. None of them spans other tests' mail, so none of them re-reads. Their behaviour is unchanged. The shared clients (`benches/mail_compare/clients/`) and their digests are unchanged.
- **Proofs.** In `evals/tests/testexec.rs`, the scripted Mailpit store now has `move_on`. On the first page of a store-wide listing with that ordinal, it stores another worker's delivery right after it answers, so the next page states a new total. The trigger is the request itself, and there are no sleeps. `a_moving_store_wide_listing_is_reread_only_beside_neighbours_and_only_in_the_deadline` uses a two-page store (260 neighbours). With neighbours, a moved recovery and a moved check are each read again, and the cleanup is `Done { removed: 6 }` with the neighbours untouched. Alone, the same move fails at once and nothing is deleted. A listing that never settles fails inside a 300 ms deadline and makes no mutation. `a_parallel_suites_cleanup_reads_a_listing_its_neighbours_moved_again` runs ten trials at 8 workers while the first ten listings move. Every row passes with proved cleanup, all 270 foreign messages remain, and no clear runs. With the re-read disabled, both tests fail. The first fails with `Failed { reason: "recover: operation: scan: incomplete" }`. The second fails with a `harness` row, `scored pass, cleanup not proved`, the host's symptom. `evals/tests/testexec.rs` now has 19 tests and takes 5.1 s.
- **Low finding.** `api_section::testexec_real_swarmail` and `api_section::testexec_real_mailpit` now carry `#[cfg_attr(not(target_os = "linux"), ignore = "bind ownership is read from /proc (Linux)")]`. Their host-proof reason moved into `#[cfg_attr(target_os = "linux", ignore = …)]`. A plain `#[ignore]` beside the marker would be a second `ignore` on other platforms, which is `unused_attributes` and fails `clippy -D warnings`. On Linux the test list shows the host-proof reason, as before.
- **Host runs (this worktree, before commit, `timeout 600 taskset -c 0-31`, the README's commands).** `testexec_real_mailpit` ran 3 times. Each exited 0 with 100 of 100 passes. The one-minute load was 60.0→67.5, 67.5→74.5 and 74.5→85.7, on a shared host where other sessions' builds were running. `testexec_real_swarmail` ran once, exit 0, 100 of 100 passes, load 90.8→97.6. These runs do not replace task 11: the dispatcher still runs each proof alone and attaches the output.
