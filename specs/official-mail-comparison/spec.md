## Continuation requirements

### C1 — Separate identity, semantics and admission

Extend the existing source lock with archive/asset versus executable digests,
digest basis, captured metadata/document hashes and attribution. Mark each required
fact observed or unresolved; a release tag or API title cannot fill a missing
source revision. Bind licenses and exact launch/cap/storage facts. Recheck bytes
at launch/finalization; changed/missing inputs refuse. Do not silently relax old
records: changed source/protocol contracts have explicit versions. Collection
requires all facts for the selected frozen profile, not merely runnable binaries.
Keep full-product prerequisites visible even when only local probes can proceed.

### C2 — Faithful bounded upstream translation

Mailpit list/search/read use `/api/v1/messages`, `/api/v1/search` and
`/api/v1/message/{ID}`. Preserve newest-first listing, encoded query/path values,
ordinary Text/HTML and read's mark-as-read side effect. Search matching completion
uses `messages_count`, not mailbox `total`; retain page `count/start` meanings.
Test unread-filter behavior before and after read. Supplied specs default pages
to 50 and do not establish a maximum; 250 is our client ceiling, not an upstream
claim. Prove page progress, duplicate-ID detection, matching totals and end-of-scan.
A short/empty/truncated or changing page that cannot establish completion is
incomplete, never an empty-success result. Offset scans during mutation are not
snapshots: stabilize post-send verification with a finite rescan budget or report
inconsistency, retaining all observations and costs.

MailHog list/search use `/api/v2/messages` and `/api/v2/search`. The supplied v2
Swagger says `messages`/lowercase `id`; panel probes saw `items`/uppercase `ID`.
Record both hashed sources, test the actual decoder and keep the discrepancy
visible. `/api/v1/messages/{id}` read still needs primary attribution and body
fidelity proof: a successful probe alone cannot resolve its missing source.

One cohesive adapter exposes only general Mailpit list/search/read and a
source-justified wait. Pin MCP 2025-03-26 with JSON results in text content blocks
as the local interoperability target used by existing eval clients. This is not
proof that final model clients negotiate it; actual #151/#152/#153 contracts remain
prerequisites. Closed schemas, bounded stdin/output/queue/in-flight work, correlated
JSON-RPC errors, cancellation and EOF are required. The plan fixes initial bounds.
No admin/send/delete/release/link-check/screenshots, task hints, extraction,
synthetic attachments, cache/index/prefetch, inbox emulation or synthetic chaos.
The existing eval owner later starts the adapter with its assigned loopback endpoint.

### C3 — Fair measurement with complete loss accounting

Keep the authored plain/HTML/multipart corpus, fresh/reused sessions, six target
orders, warmups/repetitions, 1/10/100 and bounded 1,000-session profiles, and
present/future/empty waits. Freeze method before collection; changed methods start
new runs and preserve all old runs. Record controller scheduling and one monotonic
clock. Preserve signed observation-minus-final-ack offsets, native versus adapter
versus common-client labels, request counts, send and verified-completion windows,
nearest-rank quantiles, sample counts and censored timeouts; no successes means
null quantiles. No performance threshold defines correctness.

Every planned attempt is accepted, explicitly rejected, failed before completion,
ambiguous after DATA terminator, or unstarted. Independently reconcile visible
unique IDs, duplicates, missing IDs and known evictions. A visible ambiguous
message does not prove a received 250; do not retry it into a success. No best-run
selection, dropped failures, clamped offsets or assumed competitor accept==stored.
Retention must cover cumulative residency, including waits/warmups where they
share state, with explicit global/per-inbox meaning. Mailpit default retention 500
cannot serve a 3,000-message exact-count workload. Prove finite sufficient settings
or zero/unlimited semantics; disclose compression, storage, durability and actual
resource enforcement separately. No matched-memory or fsync claim from a scratch DB.

### C4 — Own resources until observed cleanup

## Acceptance Criteria

The complete inherited AC1–AC16 follow verbatim and remain normative. C1–C5
above refine their proofs for this continuation; none is checked by design.


- **AC1 — Frozen method:** Complete committed protocol binds each run; validation
  refuses missing fields, altered hashes and post hoc subset/repetition changes.
  Setup-only outputs cannot become measurements.
- **AC2 — Real competitors:** All three targets execute with verified source/artifact/
  platform/license records. Wrong digest/version, missing server and mutable-only
  pins refuse; no silent skip or compat-shim substitute.
- **AC3 — Adapter:** Real Mailpit SMTP/HTTP/MCP tests prove ordinary list/read/search,
  body fidelity, pagination, identity limitations, chosen waits, timeout/error bounds
  and absence of fixture-admin/task-specific capabilities.
- **AC4 — Same-suite controls:** Applicable tasks retain fixture/scorer/difficulty
  and matching public inputs on both targets. All oracles pass and all nulls fail
  their declared idle class through the external path; every catalogue cell and
  unsupported/infrastructure case remains accounted.
- **AC5 — Throughput:** Real three-target runs reconcile attempts, accepts, rejects,
  ambiguity, visible/duplicate IDs; state units/windows and retain failures, corpus,
  resources, warmup and counts.
- **AC6 — Waits:** Real three-target present/future/empty tests never return unrelated
  mail; retain timeouts and signed offsets and reproduce quantiles without
  performance-based pass thresholds.
- **AC7 — Limits:** The 1,000-session profile is bounded; saturation/refusal/timeout
  remains data. Storage/cap differences are not pooled and exact-count workloads
  cannot knowingly overflow retention.
- **AC8 — Lifecycle:** Success, early failure, cancellation and death prove owned
  reap/drain/cleanup; uncertainty stops admission. Unowned resources survive and
  partial output receives no completion index.
- **AC9 — Privacy:** Benign real-process canaries prove closed environments and no
  child diagnostic/credential/private-argv leakage, private exclusive modes,
  collision refusal, bounded responses and safe rendering; existing tests retain strength.
- **AC10 — Schema/replay:** Strict native v2 behavior remains; comparison target
  typing, hashes, coordinates and replay reject mixed/foreign/duplicate/truncated
  evidence. Existing trap/provenance/concurrency proofs remain intact.
- **AC11 — One command:** Clean documented prerequisites reproduce local/integrated
  free checks and reports with one entry point; a separately authorized operator can
  rerun the full final product through its explicit measure mode. Failures identify
  the blocked phase;
  evidence mode never starts paid work or substitutes local-only success.
- **AC12 — Eval readiness:** Reconciled eval sources pass standalone fmt, locked
  clippy and full default-threaded tests, plus actual #148/#201 host-wide coordination
  and required CI receipts. Conflict markers/private locks are not proof.
- **AC13 — Genuine dossier:** docs/evals contains actual #151 baseline, actual final
  same-suite Swarmail/Mailpit trials, fresh valid controls, method, actual costs and
  safe raw data bound to exact versions/contracts/settings. Unsupported full-suite
  semantics or missing required costs/baseline leave this criterion open.
- **AC14 — Honest claims:** Every declared run/cell/loss appears with its denominator.
  Numeric README agent claims resolve to admitted data and reproducible arithmetic;
  measured business failures are evidence, not grounds to rerun.
- **AC15 — Guarantees/gates:** All house gates, exact coverage/selftest, publish/seal
  proofs, permissive licenses, fail-closed native-tls absence, benchmark build/run and
  independent eval/real-server checks have required receipts. No guarantee, historical
  spec, gate, coverage policy or dependency allowance is weakened.
- **AC16 — Delivery integrity:** Carry inherited HIGH security-relevant review-integrity
  findings unchanged into protected review; obtain original references without invented
  IDs/closure and retain #219 separately. Require the five independent mixed-route
  results and hard security hold. Source merge is not a published pinned engine.
  0090/0091 stay provisional; operator alone reconciles/accepts. No paid trial, push,
  merge, publication, tag or issue closure occurs in this delivery.
