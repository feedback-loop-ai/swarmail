# Count paid tokens through the nested driver

Decision: 0138

Part of #404, #391 and #164. The paid baseline can already authorize a product, run its controls and commit individual trial scores. Its token comparison needs the cost of failed trials as well as passes. The November 23 smoke found that the outer exec adapter consumes the nested driver's stdout. A successful driver process can even exit without launching a CLI. Reading the journal or using process exit alone would mismeasure the models.

The wrapper will act as the engine for the nested driver. Only the harness may attach its typed session usage to the scored trial. The runner retains ownership of cancellation, cleanup and grading. The proposed record defines how a reader recognizes committed accounting evidence. [Proposed decision 0138](../../docs/decisions/0138-nested-driver-usage.md)
