# Prove MCP chunk cleanup before sealing

Issue #385 closes the gap in story B’s AC8. The holder can attest to its target leaf, but cannot witness the end of its caller. A direct adapter can exit successfully while a child survives the process-group signal. The collector needs evidence from outside the workload before it can admit the measurement. This implements the operator’s existing ruling under the pinned-host trust model. [Decision 0132, including the November 18 and 19 addenda](../../docs/decisions/0132-mail-comparison-protocol-v4.md) · [API latency contract](../api-latency/spec.md)

## Acceptance Criteria

1. On every collect-v4 MCP coordinate, including direct Swarmail, establish a fresh non-delegated client scope before api-mcp executes. The in-process bridge and every adapter and ordinary descendant inherit it across fork, exec, setsid and reparenting. Only the holder enters its separate delegated driver scope and the target occupies its measured leaf. The observer runs outside both. The existing holder-only placement regression passes.
2. After normal exit, measured failure, startup failure, timeout, control loss or handled interruption, the owner judges the client scope before releasing the coordinate. A positive receipt requires a matching launch identity and observed recursive emptiness or verified removal of that established cgroup. Missing, stale, malformed, inaccessible or ambiguous evidence yields infrastructure exit 2. Non-Linux refuses the checked launch before spawning a workload.
3. Start the client judgment immediately upon direct command completion or forced cutoff. Its observation and diagnostics together have one monotonic deadline of at most five seconds, clipped to the remaining chunk budget. A populated scope at first observation is a survivor even if it empties later. The complete launch, both judgments and containment fit 0132’s 600 seconds. No wait for a survivor to exit, inherited pipe EOF, unbounded manager response or fresh per-member time allowance.
4. A survivor yields exit 2, one attempt, no next coordinate, and neither a public chunk index nor a collection seal. The incomplete run’s durable per-coordinate report and operator diagnostic name the unit, observation and available survivor PID/process names. Record truncation, identity races or diagnostic timeout explicitly. A subsequent kill or successful stop cannot erase the original finding. Unknown cleanup reports its specific cause.
5. Publish an MCP completion index only after valid holder receipt, target leaf removal, clean holder scope, affirmative client evidence and an admissible workload status. Preserve the measured distinction between exits 0 and 1. V4 admission rejects a missing, foreign or unsuccessful client receipt on an MCP coordinate. An observer crash before proof leaves an incomplete run. Preserve the existing native and historical admission contracts.
6. Deterministic Linux tests run a real fake adapter whose acknowledged descendant outlives shutdown, and a matched clean control. With valid holder evidence in both, observe the child alive when failure is reported, and prove the clean case seals. A controlled stalled probe proves the total deadline without a real protocol-length wait. A portable refusal test exercises the non-Linux decision.
7. Each of the three existing bridged dispatcher proofs exercises the collection judgment and asserts positive client evidence as well as existing placement, admission and measurement assertions, including before the accepted MailSlurper fill-shortfall return. Local proof includes discovery and explicit missing-prerequisite failure. Dispatcher execution and PR logs remain open until run individually on verified bytes. Story E collection waits for this proof.
