## Feature and rationale

Make the accepted Codex review contract an executable, required proof owned by
the pinned delivery workflow. The existing Python proof rejects accepted fast
Luna implementation before dispatch, uses tautological negatives and has no
required caller. A green house inventory therefore does not establish its
outstanding routing, aggregation or identity obligations.

One canonical proof must resolve actual recipes, exercise the recorded engine
and distinguish intended refusals from fixture crashes. Receipts must bind
the outer candidate and tooling. Historical foreign-attempt acceptance remains
a separate security obligation: repair and prove it through its owner, or stop
with exact unresolved evidence. A clean individual review cannot cancel the
supplied aggregate has_security_residual=true.

This is engine/high-risk delivery work. Accepted
[0050](../../docs/decisions/0050-delivery-gates.md),
[0057](../../docs/decisions/0057-codex-delivery-recipes.md) and
[0058](../../docs/decisions/0058-codex-review-routing.md) are authorities.
0062 proposes required proof ownership and evidence details as a supplement;
it neither changes the reviewer matrix nor accepts itself. Compatible
restoration follows [0045 §8](../../docs/decisions/0045-delivery-repairs.md);
contradictory semantics wait for operator acceptance.

## User scenarios

1. Recipe drift is detected by real resolution and independent expectations;
   correct Luna/xhigh passes and reaches behavioral controls.
2. Adverse or incomplete reviews retain their facts through production
   aggregation and reach the exact protected outcome, alongside valid positive
   clean and safe-medium contrasts.
3. Candidate audits distinguish current evidence from history and cannot turn
   missing, interrupted or unrelated results into ship permission.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1 — Exact resolution.** Recorded-engine resolution of self, codex,
  codex-careful and every codex-fast selector matches independent literals for
  offices, roles, provider/model routes, efforts and ordered chains. Standard
  implementation stays Sol/high, Careful Sol/xhigh; all four fast lanes use
  the named fast office on Luna/xhigh; fast triage stays Sol/medium. Legacy
  self retains its four-role review and authorized chains. Unknown/absent fast
  selection refuses without default. Check official Flash by resolved route,
  not alias. Astra offices and independent Fable remain distinct.
- **AC2 — Falsifiable oracle.** Actual matrices and malformed variants enter
  one checker. Missing/extra role, wrong office/provider/implementer/effort,
  swapped security route, merged Astra identities, absent independent member,
  Pro and unauthorized fallback each yield the intended diagnostic. Unknown
  selection uses the real resolver. Unrelated resolution failure cannot pass
  a matrix case; inequality comparisons prove nothing.
- **AC3 — Real dispatch.** Controlled offline participants drive production
  recorded dispatch using compositions connected to resolved definitions for
  1/2/3/5 roles; retain legacy-self preservation checks. Assert calls, identities,
  results and failure classifications. Only supported pre-session Qwen failure
  in fast-feature correctness selects Sonnet. Completed verdicts, malformed
  facts, accepted/checkpointed sessions and timeout/watchdog outcomes cannot
  silently substitute a reviewer. Flash/high-risk roles have no fallback.
  Exhaustion reaches bounded refusal; call counts follow proven engine
  semantics, not the existing guessed literal. No third model or Pro in Codex
  paths. Controlled participants do not prove live-provider compatibility.
- **AC4 — Aggregation and genuine returns.** Execute production aggregation
  and policy for clean, safe medium non-security, high, security, hold and
  unknown facts, including absent/null/wrong-typed safety, scope and fixes.
  Assert typed aggregate, rule ID and destination. Any member's witnessed
  security survives a clean security member; Fable affects worst verdict.
  Missing/merged members refuse. Security alone owns panel hold; chore retains
  combined authority. Observe actual scope/fixes returns and bounded exhaustion
  through real targets or a harness linked to recorded core. Terminalized
  fixtures and arbitrary non-completion cannot establish these transitions.
- **AC5 — Identity closure or stop.** Defensive owner tests establish active
  run/effect/attempt/member/result and candidate correlation before
  accepted/checkpoint/result events authorize transitions. Cover legitimate
  completion/retries/resume and stale/foreign/replayed/swapped identities and
  candidate changes on affected single/panel paths. Bound positive controls
  must succeed. Preserve historical red evidence; closure requires reviewed
  source, recorded-engine regressions and candidate-bound receipts. If faithful
  proof or bounded repair is unavailable, retain security=true and stop with
  available expected/observed identities, engine identity, trace, missing
  evidence and corrected bounded proposal. Stop is a safe disposition, not
  feature completion or identity closure.
- **AC6 — Required pinned ownership.** Algorithm/helper closure runs from
  dispatched pinned resources. Inherited verify requires ordinary routing
  proof before pass; existing CI requires the same canonical algorithm after
  source/patch/build identity checks. Explicit candidate/pinned roots prevent
  mount-relative confusion. Candidate configuration is assessed data, never
  a selector of verifier code. Missing resources/unrecorded engines refuse.
  Update the single house inventory.
- **AC7 — Workflow refusals.** Required outer routing-dispatch executes actual
  inherited verify: broken proof, missing/tampered resource, changed public
  shim and unrecorded engine cannot yield pass; restoration does pass. Inspect
  typed result and process status: exit zero can carry fail. Rerun outer
  eval-dispatch and artifact provenance controls. Neither outer mode runs
  inside ordinary verify. Integrated timeout stays incomplete even when
  components passed.
- **AC8 — Fresh binding.** Every case records full outer HEAD/tree, dirty state
  and deliberate mutation digest; distinct fixture identity; proof/helper
  hashes; engine base/binary/ordered patches; recipe/strategy and
  role/run/effect/attempt/result identities; command, exit/timeout, typed
  result/aggregate and decisive trace. Use fresh isolated outputs, bounded
  processes and atomic per-case/final receipts. Pass requires the complete
  required-case set and matching bindings. Stale/partial/mismatched evidence
  cannot fill a missing case. Preserve failures/history; rerun after interruption
  rather than implementing reuse. Candidate changes invalidate earlier claims.
- **AC9 — Disposition and documentation.** Every packet row below gets a final
  evidence-linked disposition retaining severity/provenance. Correct both 0058
  summaries to operator acceptance on 2026-10-24. Restore eval commands, agent
  contract, result schema and failure taxonomy checked against current CLI/code;
  retain accepted 0055, actual gate/artifact guidance and honest limits.
  Reconcile routing T1–T8 and eval handoff by dated criteria-to-receipt entries.
  Recover authentic originals through authorized access or preserve exact gaps
  without claiming inspection, external correction or full source reconciliation.
- **AC10 — Final verification.** Every gate in the
  [single inventory](../../docs/house-rules.md#gates--part-of-every-change)
  and repaired proof passes on the new exact candidate. Preserve locked builds,
  root-test-artifact selection and inherited SWARMAIL_BIN replacement, full
  eval suite, exact 100/100/100 coverage/selftest, publish/seal proofs,
  license/TLS checks and pinned design/delivery proofs. Offline verify installs
  nothing; CI-only checks stay CI-owned. The 487818c pass cannot certify a new
  candidate.
- **AC11 — Review/authority.** After verification obtain five distinct
  candidate-bound results: Astra correctness, official DeepSeek Flash security,
  Sonnet 5.5 tests, Astra guarantees, Fable 5.1 independent. No Pro, fallback,
  author substitution or missing-role acceptance. Actual aggregation preserves
  adverse/unknown/security facts and hard hold. Later fixes require fresh
  applicable verification/review. No push, merge, tag, publication, issue
  closure or decision acceptance. Chief design commit is expressly requested;
  later unsigned implementation commits remain subject to the user's
  policy-reaches-ship restriction.

decision, corrected prose or previous gates are not implementation completion,
security clearance or shipping permission.
