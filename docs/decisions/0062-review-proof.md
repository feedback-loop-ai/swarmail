## Context

## Proposed ruling

1. One canonical proof under bundles/self/scripts is required by inherited
   pinned verify before pass and existing recorded-engine CI. Its executable
   helper closure, policy and tooling expectations are pinned resources.
   Candidate recipes/offices/adapters are assessed data. Explicit roots replace
   location-derived trust. Remove the orphan tests entry after moving its
   algorithm; no compatibility shim is required.
2. Actual resolution uses independent accepted literals; real and malformed
   matrices enter the same checker. Behavioral proofs execute recorded
   production dispatch/aggregation/evaluator and genuine returns, asserting
   identities, facts, rules and destinations. Generic failure, compilation,
   source shape and local policy clones are insufficient. Controlled
   participants prove engine behavior, not live-provider compatibility.
3. Ordinary proof runs inside verify. Required outer routing-dispatch proves
   actual verifier refusal/restoration for proof/resource/shim/engine defects.
   Outer eval-dispatch remains required for this repair. Neither outer mode
   recurses through verify. Typed JSON and process outcome both matter;
   exit zero with result fail is a refusal.
4. Evidence binds full outer candidate and distinct fixture, proof/helper
   digests, engine source/binary/ordered patches, recipe/strategy and
   run/effect/attempt/member/result identities to exact transitions.
   Atomic per-case receipts preserve failure/timeout evidence. A fresh complete
   required-case summary is necessary; stale, partial, missing or mismatched
   evidence never authorizes delivery. No reuse service is introduced.
5. Identity correlation belongs to the engine owner and is distinct from
   candidate binding. Defensive positive/refusal owner tests must establish
   current behavior. Necessary bounded repair uses reviewed source, ordered
   patches and reproducible tooling identity. Unavailable proof/source/build
   or live unresolved failure retains security status and stops completion
   with exact evidence and corrected bounded proposal. No consumer waiver,
   pin bypass or expanded fallback is authorized.
6. Fresh full gates and accepted five-role high-risk review apply to the final
   candidate. Every packet fact has explicit disposition. Current summaries
   cite existing acceptance while preserving historical accepted bodies.
   Operator alone accepts this record; compatible restoration follows 0045 §8,
   contradictions wait for acceptance.

## Alternatives and consequences

A Luna-only edit leaves ineffective/unexecuted controls; reject it.
A second oracle, copied verifier or evidence framework adds trust boundaries;
reuse existing owners. An old-path wrapper creates integrity maintenance
without a required caller; accept changing manual commands. Reuse/resume is
deferred, so interrupted proofs rerun. Exact traces, distinct Astra results,
independent Fable and full verification cost time but remain required.

No application runtime, dependency/license policy, coverage denominator,
provider route or accepted guarantee changes. A still-unsafe candidate may
stop; that is never feature completion.
